## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Chaos Front**. This trainer provides optional control over campaign resources, faction progression, unit survivability, and combat outcomes while preserving Chaos Front's mercenary management, tactical battles, and branching war decisions.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore Chaos Front's campaign branches, recruit and develop a larger mercenary corps, test different faction strategies, or reduce the difficulty of tactical battles.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Chaos Front is organized around movement across the Centersea Sector world map rather than direct character traversal. Move the mercenary corps between mission centers, faction areas, and active war zones, then select available missions or participate in battles initiated by major powers.

### Tools & Inventory

Manage the corps through its available credits, recruited personnel, and military assets. The game includes spaceships, mecha, artillery, fighters, submarines, and other units with different weapons and abilities. Use credits to buy and modify equipment, and use faction merit to exchange for exclusive ships and ARMS when the required relationship level has been reached.

### World & Environment

The campaign takes place during the Centersea War, where mercenaries can support competing galactic powers, the Revolutionary Army, or the Black Flag. Missions, faction battles, relationships, and strategic proposals influence the developing conflict. Allied factions can receive proposals focused on governance, subversion, or war, with feasible plans prioritized during the next strategic phase.

### Combat & Weapons

Battles use turn-based tactical rules and a varied unit roster. Units can employ stealth, space mines, fog, capture, theft, ramming, shelling, shields, defensive force fields, and direct attacks. Position ships, mecha, artillery, fighters, and other units carefully, extension their distinct abilities, and protect key assets until the mission objectives are complete.
